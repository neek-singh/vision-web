2. **Institute 2025 me shuru hua.** "Over two decades", "pioneer of IT education" wali lines hatani hain. Sahi line: "Since 2025".
3. **Certificate Drishti Computer Education ka hai.** Site pe saaf likho ki Vision IT unki franchise branch hai. Isse trust badhta hai, aur galat expectation nahi banti.
4. **Sirf 4 courses chal rahe hain: BCC, DCA, ADCA, PGDCA.** Home page ki list (Tally, DTP, Photoshop, Typing, Web Development, Python, AI Tools) aur `/courses` ke 45 skill cards **hatao**. Inme se jo cheezein DCA/ADCA/PGDCA ke andar module ke roop me padhate ho, wo sirf module ke roop me dikhao.
5. **Basic Computer for Beginner page** (`/courses/basic-beginner`): ye alag course nahi chal raha. Isko hatao aur `/courses/bcc` pe 301 redirect do.
6. **Placement 0 hai, students ka count confirm nahi.** "Placement", "20+ students", "hundreds of alumni" sab hatao. Sirf "Resume building aur interview preparation" likho (agar aap sach me karate ho).
